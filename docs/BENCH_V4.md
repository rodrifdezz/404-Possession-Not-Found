# v4 ablation bench (experimental)

Generated by `python -m bench.v4 --report` from 5 of 5 outer folds. Nothing here uses the challenge match; the submitted model (v3) and the submission file are unchanged.

## Protocol

* 10 external matches: 3 Metrica sample matches (labels derived from their events) and 7 IDSSE / DFL Bundesliga matches (official per-frame flags). Downloaded locally, never committed.
* 5 outer folds of 2 whole matches: {M1, J03WMX}, {M2, J03WN1}, {M3, J03WOH}, {J03WOY, J03WPY}, {J03WQQ, J03WR9}. A model is only ever scored on matches it was not fitted on, in either layer.
* Every held-out match is degraded exactly as in `bench/run.py` (short and long ball gaps x seeds 0 and 1, seeds never used for training) and scored with the official `possession_metrics.py` against both readings of the truth (`bench/targets.py`). Identical inputs for every candidate: the comparisons are paired.
* Decoder unchanged: Viterbi with the v3 costs (switch 6, dead 48).
* **V3** = the stored model on DFL matches; on a Metrica match the same recipe fitted on the two other Metrica matches (as in `BENCH_MODEL_CV.md`).
* **V4-A..D** = fitted on the 8 other matches (Metrica + DFL), 7 training degradations, 1 frame in 6 (the same number of rows as v3, from more matches). Layer 2 is fitted on out-of-sample layer-1 probabilities from 3 inner groups of whole matches. V4-B adds the possession-change features to layer 2 (`bench/v4_stack.py`); V4-C adds the touch features to layer 1 (`src/touch_features.py`); V4-D both.
* Extra measures: short possessions (< 5 s and < 2 s) recovered at IoU >= 0.5; false H<->A switches = predicted direct changes of team with no true change towards that team within +-2 s, per 90 min.
* Adoption rule fixed before the results (`bench/v4_report.py`): mean paired spellF1 difference > 0 overall and in each provider, wins on >= 70% of the matches, no match worse by more than 0.03, false switches up by at most 20%; the simplest adopted candidate wins unless a richer one is better by more than 0.005.
